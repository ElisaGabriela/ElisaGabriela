### 👩‍💻 About Me

I'm a Computer Engineer and PhD candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the **Federal University of Rio Grande do Norte (UFRN), Brazil**.

My research focuses on the intersection of **High-Performance Computing (HPC)** and **Artificial Intelligence**, particularly Machine Learning techniques for performance prediction and scalability analysis of parallel applications.

I am interested in developing intelligent solutions that help understand, evaluate, and optimize the performance of parallel computing systems.

Currently, I also work as a **Systems Analyst** at the State Department of Public Health of Rio Grande do Norte (SESAP).

### 🚀 Research & Projects

**PaScal Suite – Parallel Scalability Analysis**

Research and development of Machine Learning models to accelerate the scalability analysis of parallel applications.

- Predictive performance modeling.
- Adaptive sampling strategies.
- Machine Learning integration into HPC analysis tools.
- Performance evaluation using parallel benchmarks.

**Machine Learning for Fault Diagnosis**

Investigation of Machine Learning algorithms with different computational complexities for fault diagnosis in sucker rod pumping systems, in collaboration with LAUT and WEG.
